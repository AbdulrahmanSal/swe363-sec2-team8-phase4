## Visual direction

- KFUPM Green `#008540`, Forest `#00573F`, Petrol `#003E51`, restrained Gold `#DAC961`.
- White header, forest sidebar, pale gray-green workspace, white bordered panels, gold selected-menu rule.
- Clear screen/requirement labels and the original admin workflows.
- Arial/Helvetica fallback with no network fonts required. Official branding also lists Poppins; use it only when installed/licensed through the university's brand resources.
- Custom MH mark, NOT an official university seal. This is a student project, not an official KFUPM service.

Brand reference: https://mediacenter.kfupm.edu.sa/documents/15/KFUPM_Style_Board.pdf
